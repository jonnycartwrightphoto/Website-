About

Rooted in rural life

I grew up on working land. That means I understand the 4am starts, the seasonal rhythms, and the quiet pride in what you've built — and I know how to photograph it without making it look like a stock image.

My work is for farms, market gardens, rural makers, agritourism businesses, and anyone whose brand lives outdoors. If you want images that actually look like your place, let's talk.

What I offer

Shoots built around your season

◈

Brand Day

A full day on your property. We capture the breadth of your business — landscapes, portraits, products, and process — in one session.

◇

Seasonal Set

Four half-day visits across the year. Your imagery stays current and shows the honest change of your land through the seasons.

◉

Product & Market

Focused shoots for market stalls, online shops, and packaging. Clean, honest product photography that sells without pretending.

◎

Portraits

You, your family, your team — on your land, in your work clothes. Environmental portraits that tell the truth about who you are.

How it works

Simple from start to finish

First

Enquire

Fill in the booking form below. Tell me about your business, your land, and what you're hoping to use the images for.

Then

Plan Together

We'll talk through timing (season matters), locations on your property, and the kind of story you want to tell.

Shoot Day

Show Up as You Are

No styling, no staging. We work with what's real. The best shots happen when you're just doing what you do.

Delivery

Images in Hand

Edited gallery delivered within two weeks. Formatted for web, print, and social. Yours to use however you need.
